My path to vacation rental management is rooted in experience that drives results. With 20 years in digital advertising, I know how to position properties, attract the right guests, and maximize bookings.

I later became a health coach, which shaped my approach to creating spaces that feel calm, welcoming, and memorable—earning consistent five-star reviews.

Today, I bring that combined expertise to the Kona luxury rental market. I've worked with Fortune 500 brands like Paramount Pictures, Target, and Toyota, and I apply that same level of strategy, professionalism, and attention to detail to every home I manage.

As a Kona resident, HOA board member, and Airbnb Superhost, I understand both the market and the community. I offer a hands-on, boutique approach that delivers strong revenue, exceptional guest experiences, and peace of mind for homeowners.

An Important Difference

Co-Hosting vs. Traditional
Property Management

While traditional property management focuses on managing many properties at once, boutique co-hosting is intentionally smaller—allowing for thoughtful oversight and attention to detail for each home. With Co-Hosting, you own the listing, reviews, and the reputation you build on Airbnb and VRBO.

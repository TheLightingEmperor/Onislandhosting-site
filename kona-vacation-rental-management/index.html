Questions

Before We Talk

Do I keep my Airbnb listing?

Yes. I'm added as a co-host on your existing Airbnb listing. Your account, guest reviews, Superhost history and payouts all stay in your name.

How is a co-host different from Kona vacation rental management?

If you've been searching for Kona vacation rental management or Kona Airbnb management, it helps to know the difference. A traditional management company often lists your home under its own account and collects the rental income before paying you. As your Airbnb co-host in Kona, I work inside your listing instead. Airbnb pays you directly, shares my agreed co-host fee automatically, and you can see every booking and payout in real time.

What does Airbnb co-hosting in Kona cost?

My co-host fee is agreed with you upfront and paid automatically through Airbnb from each booking, so there are no separate invoices. On your complimentary call I'll walk you through the details and what your home could earn.

I live on the mainland. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Off-island owners are exactly who I built On Island Hosting for. I become your eyes and hands in Kona, from guest arrivals to vendor visits, and keep you updated so you never have to wonder how your home is doing.

Do you work with long-term rentals?

No. I co-host short-term and vacation rentals only, which lets me give each home and every guest my full attention.
